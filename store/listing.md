## Description [4000]
Captain Blastroid is a vertical shoot-'em-up made for a phone held in one hand. Drag anywhere to fly. Your ship fires on its own. Everything else is up to you.

EVERY RUN IS THREE MINUTES
Pick a sector, launch, and build an arsenal as you go. Capsules drop new weapons and level up the ones you carry: blasters, spread shots, piercing lances, seeker pods and orbit drones. Chain kills for score, grab the salvage, and bring down the boss at the end.

FIVE SECTORS, FIVE BOSSES
Thread the Kessler Belt. Ride out tractor beams in the Ion Shoals. Slingshot around black holes at the Event Horizon. Hide from pulsar sweeps in the Pulsar Nursery. Then take on the Armada and its flagship, the Leviathan.

A HANGAR WORTH FILLING
Salvage carries over between runs. Find schematics and build seven ships, from the armored Bulwark to the five-slot Paragon. Rescue escape pods to recruit seven pilots, each with a different edge. Buy hangar upgrades that stick.

ACT II, FREE
Clear the Armada and claim the Act II expansion at no cost: five more sectors with solar flares, cave tunnels, portals and time bubbles, five more ships and pilots, three new weapons, and the Gauntlet, an endgame run through eight bosses back to back.

NO NONSENSE
- No ads. No accounts. No tracking.
- Plays offline.
- Portrait, one thumb, with haptics.
- Hand-made 16-bit style pixel art and a chiptune soundtrack.
- Export a save code to move your progress to another device.

## Review notes (App Store)
No login is needed. The Act II expansion is free and unlocks inside the app with no purchase: Hangar → SECTOR → swipe to ACT II → tap a card → GET FREE. Its sectors then open one at a time as the one before is cleared.
