Captain Blastroid: Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

Captain Blastroid is published by 302 Comedy LLC. This policy covers the iOS app, the Android app and the web version.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The game has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information.

The game does count a few milestones so we know how many people reach them: opening the game for the first time, finishing Act I, viewing an expansion, getting an expansion, and playing it. When you reach one, the game sends a single message containing the name of the milestone, whether you are on iOS, Android or the web, and the game's version number. The message carries no user, device or advertising identifier, and each milestone is sent once. Our server adds one to a daily total and keeps nothing else, so the totals cannot be traced back to a person or a device. They are not shared with or sold to anyone.

The totals are kept on Supabase, our hosting provider. As with any internet request, its servers see your IP address while handling the message; we do not store it with the counts or use it.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (salvage, ships, pilots, upgrades, high scores) and your settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in a browser, deletes them. A save code you export from Settings is a copy of that progress that you choose to make; it goes wherever you paste it and nowhere else.

Purchases

If the game offers a paid expansion, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details; the store only tells the app whether the expansion is owned.

The web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when the page loads; see GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or use those logs.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The milestone counts described above are the same for every player.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
